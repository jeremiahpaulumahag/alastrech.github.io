# AlaStretch - Quick Start Guide

**Status**: ✅ COMPLETE & READY  
**Access**: http://localhost:3000 (server should be running)

## What You Have

✅ **Interactive 4-minute stretch workout**  
✅ **3 personalized animated characters** (Ala, Ruby, Tine)  
✅ **8 exercises** (5 seated, 3 standing)  
✅ **Full audio system** (music, speech, sounds)  
✅ **Voice control** (optional mic commands)  
✅ **Video export ready** (needs FFmpeg)

## Quick Test

1. Open browser to `http://localhost:3000`
2. Click "▶ Start Workout"
3. Watch 3 characters perform synchronized stretches
4. Listen to background music and exercise announcements
5. See countdown timer and progress bar
